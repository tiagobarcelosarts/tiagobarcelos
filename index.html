Unseen
Presence

2026

A man sits on the ground, reading. Around him, the city moves on — people pass absorbed in their routines, barely noticing his presence. Beneath him, the pavement unfolds into pages, turning the street into a space suspended between survival and thought.

The figure remains present, yet almost unseen, held between movement and stillness. What emerges is not only absence, but a fragile persistence.

About

A Brazilian artist
observing Ireland

Tiago Barcelos' artistic expression is deeply connected to urban life and everyday experience, shaped by a critical observation of the spaces, structures and contradictions that define human life. As a Brazilian immigrant living in Ireland, his work emerges from a constant tension between displacement and belonging.

Moving between such different realities has sharpened his attention to the city, to social dynamics, and to the quiet details of daily life. Through layered brushstrokes and restrained compositions, his work reveals both the extraordinary and the contradictions within what often appears ordinary.

His scenes do not seek to resolve these tensions, but to hold them — allowing presence, vulnerability and memory to surface slowly.
